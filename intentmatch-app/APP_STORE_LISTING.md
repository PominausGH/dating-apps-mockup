## Description
IntentMatch is a dating app designed for people who are serious about finding meaningful connections. Unlike traditional dating apps, IntentMatch focuses on scheduling real dates rather than endless messaging.

**Key Features:**

🗓️ **Availability-Based Matching**
Share your free time slots and match with people who are actually available when you are. No more "when are you free?" conversations.

💬 **24-Hour Chat Windows**
Once matched, you have 24 hours to schedule a date. This keeps conversations focused and moving forward.

📍 **Venue Suggestions**
Get personalized venue recommendations based on your location and preferences. From coffee shops to dinner spots, find the perfect place for your first date.

⭐ **Accountability Scores**
Build your reputation by showing up to dates. Higher accountability scores mean more visibility and better matches.

🔒 **Privacy First**
Your data is secure. Control who sees your profile and when.

**Why IntentMatch?**
- Designed for people ready to date, not just chat
- Reduces ghosting with accountability features
- Makes scheduling dates effortless
- Quality over quantity approach to matching

Download IntentMatch and start dating with intention.
