## Subtitle (iOS) / Short Description (Android)

## Description
VoiceFirst is a revolutionary dating app where you connect through voice before photos. Hear someone's personality before you see their face, creating more authentic connections.

**Key Features:**

🎙️ **Voice Intros**
Record a 5-30 second voice intro that showcases your personality. Let your voice make the first impression.

👂 **Listen First, See Later**
Browse profiles by listening to voice intros. Photos are progressively revealed as you connect, reducing superficial swiping.

🔓 **Progressive Photo Unlock**
Photos blur decreases as you exchange voice messages. By the time you see each other clearly, you've already connected on a deeper level.

💜 **Voice Messages**
No typing needed. Send voice messages to keep conversations natural and engaging.

🎭 **Voice Verification**
Verify your voice to build trust. Verified profiles get more matches.

**Why VoiceFirst?**
- Reduces superficial swiping
- Emphasizes personality over looks
- Creates more meaningful first impressions
- Encourages authentic conversations
- Voice verification prevents catfishing

**How It Works:**
1. Record your voice intro (5-30 seconds)
2. Browse profiles by listening to intros
3. Like voices that resonate with you
4. Match and send voice messages
5. Photos unlock as you connect

Download VoiceFirst and let your voice be heard.
