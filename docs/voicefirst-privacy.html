VoiceFirst Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

VoiceFirst ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Account Information: Email address, name, date of birth, and gender
  • Voice Data: Voice intro recordings (5-30 seconds), voice messages
  • Profile Photos: Photos you upload (revealed progressively)
  • Profile Data: Bio, interests, and preferences
  • Location Data: General location for matching (city-level)
  • Communications: Voice and text messages with other users

Voice Recording Notice

VoiceFirst is a voice-first dating app. Your voice recordings are a core feature of the service and will be shared with potential matches. Voice data is stored securely and used solely for matching and communication purposes.

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information (type, operating system)
  • App usage data and analytics
  • Log data and crash reports

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To match you with compatible users based on voice intros and preferences
  • To facilitate voice and text communication between matched users
  • To progressively reveal photos as connections deepen
  • To verify user authenticity through voice
  • To improve our services and user experience
  • To send important notifications about your account and matches

4. Progressive Photo Reveal

VoiceFirst uses a progressive photo reveal system. Photos are only shown to matches after certain interaction milestones are reached. This is designed to encourage connections based on personality rather than appearance alone.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information:

  • With other users as part of the matching and communication features (voice intros, messages, progressive photo reveals)
  • With service providers who assist in operating our app
  • When required by law or to protect our rights
  • In connection with a business transfer or merger

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • Encryption of voice recordings and messages
  • Secure storage of photos
  • Encrypted data transmission
  • Regular security assessments

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data including voice recordings
  • Delete your voice intro and re-record
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications

8. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, we will delete your personal data, including all voice recordings and photos, within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

9. Children's Privacy

VoiceFirst is intended for users 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or via email.
